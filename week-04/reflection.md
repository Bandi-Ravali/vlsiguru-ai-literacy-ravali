# Week 04: Final Reflection

## What I Learned

During Week 04, I learned about the importance of data in deep learning. I understood how raw data is collected, cleaned, preprocessed, labelled and divided into training, validation and testing datasets.

I learned the differences between artificial intelligence, machine learning and deep learning. I also explored the structure of a neural network, including input layers, hidden layers, output layers, weights, biases and activation functions.

The training process helped me understand forward propagation, loss calculation, backpropagation and optimization. I learned why overfitting can reduce performance on unseen data and why testing is necessary.

The TensorFlow Playground experiment helped me investigate how changing network layers and neurons affects learning. I also explored a possible application of deep learning in semiconductor wafer defect detection.

## Key Takeaways

- Data quality is important for reliable model development.
- Deep learning models learn patterns from examples.
- Network architecture can influence learning.
- Training performance alone does not prove generalization.
- Unseen-data evaluation is important.
- Engineering applications require careful evaluation and human oversight.

## Future Learning Goals

I want to practise more neural network experiments, improve my understanding of model evaluation and explore additional AI applications in semiconductor engineering.
