# Mission 5: Experiment and Observe

## 1. Introduction

TensorFlow Playground is an interactive website for exploring how neural networks learn patterns from data. It allows learners to change the dataset, hidden layers, neurons and training settings without writing code.

Tool: https://playground.tensorflow.org/

## 2. Objective

To investigate how changes in neural network architecture affect learning and the decision boundary.

## 3. Experiment Procedure

1. Open TensorFlow Playground.
2. Select a dataset and examine its pattern.
3. Observe the initial neural network configuration.
4. Start training and record the initial behaviour.
5. Increase the number of neurons in a hidden layer.
6. Train the model again and record the changes.
7. Add or change a hidden layer and repeat.
8. Compare the results while keeping other settings as similar as possible.
9. Capture screenshots of the configurations and results.

## 4. Understanding the Decision Boundary

A decision boundary separates regions of the input space that the model assigns to different classes.

A simple dataset may need a simple boundary, while a complex dataset may require a model capable of representing more complicated patterns.

A more complex boundary does not always mean better performance. It may fit the training data too closely and generalize poorly.

## 6. Learning Outcomes

The experiment can help demonstrate that:
- Network architecture influences the patterns a model can learn.
- Training settings can affect learning behaviour.
- More neurons or layers do not guarantee better generalization.
- Actual results should be recorded rather than assumed.
